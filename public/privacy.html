Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Stays on your device

All habit and log data is stored locally. Nothing is sent to a server.

No accounts

No sign-in, no email, no password. Ever.

Anonymous analytics

Aggregate page views only. You are never identified.

No ads, no tracking

No third-party trackers, ad networks, or profiling.

Data stored on your device

Local only · never transmitted

Habit data

Your habit names, schedules, and completion logs are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to Beacon or any third party.

Local only · never transmitted

Your name

If you enter your name during setup, it is stored locally on your device only — used to personalise your greeting. It is not collected, shared, or stored elsewhere.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time from Settings → Delete all data. Clearing your browser storage or uninstalling the app also removes everything completely.

Analytics

Anonymous · aggregate only

Vercel Analytics

Beacon is hosted on Vercel and uses Vercel Analytics to understand general usage patterns — such as how many people open the app. This data is anonymous and aggregate. No personal information is collected, and no individual user is identified or tracked across sessions.

What Vercel may collect

Anonymous page views, general geographic region (country level), and device type. Vercel does not use cookies or fingerprinting for this purpose. See Vercel's Privacy Policy for full details.

What we will never do

No selling of data

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party for commercial purposes.

No advertising

Beacon contains no advertising and uses no ad networks or tracking pixels.

No behavioural profiling

We do not build profiles of individual users, track your habits across sessions, or use your data to infer personal characteristics.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the revision. Continued use of Beacon after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
